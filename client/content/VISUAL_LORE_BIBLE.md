## World Setting — "The Rustlands"

Centuries after the AI Singularity War, the world is a scorched wasteland of irradiated deserts, collapsed megacities, and half-buried server farms still humming with corrupted machine intelligence. Humanity clings to survival in scrap-built settlements, scavenging pre-war tech from the ruins of the AI overlords. Radiation poisons the land — some it kills, others it transforms. Roaming the wastes are rogue machines running on fractured code, mutant beasts fused with metal, and human fanatics who worship the dormant AI gods, hoping to reignite the Singularity.

**Art Direction:** Gritty post-apocalyptic sci-fi. Think *Mad Max* meets *Horizon Zero Dawn* meets *Slay the Spire*. Muted earth tones (rust, sand, ash) punctuated by vivid neon accents (radioactive green, plasma blue, warning orange). Characters should feel hand-built, scrappy, and lived-in — nothing is pristine. All sprites should be **front-facing, roughly 128–256 px, transparent background, pixel-art or painterly card-game style** (match to your chosen generator).

### 1. Didi — Rustborn Sharpshooter

**Role:** Ranged DPS / Containment

#### Lore

Didi was born in the ruins of Sector 7-G, an old AI weapons research facility. She grew up crawling through collapsed labs, teaching herself optics and ballistics from shattered technical manuals. By age sixteen she had jury-rigged her first sniper rifle from a security turret barrel, a car door stock, and a cracked AI targeting monocle that still whispers trajectory data in a dead machine language she half understands. She's quiet, precise, and unsettlingly calm under fire — the kind of person who counts her breaths between shots. She sells her skills to caravans and rebel outfits alike, but her real obsession is hunting the rogue AI installations that still terrorize the wastes. Every kill is personal: her mother was taken by a Sentinel sweep when Didi was nine.

#### Visual Description

- **Build:** Lean and wiry, medium height. Athletic but not bulky — built for long hours in a sniper perch.
- **Skin:** Sun-weathered olive-brown, faint radiation scars on her left cheekbone (thin luminous green lines, like cracked glass).
- **Hair:** Choppy, asymmetrical dark auburn hair, kept short on the right side and longer on the left, partially tucked under a tattered scarf/bandana.
- **Eyes:** One natural (dark brown), one replaced by a glowing cyan AI-optic monocle bolted into the eye socket with crude brass screws. The optic hums faintly and projects a faint blue targeting reticle when active.
- **Outfit:** Layered scavenger gear — a sleeveless armored vest made from stitched-together drone plating (matte gunmetal with faded warning stencils), wrapped forearms with copper-wire bracelets that serve as tool storage, cargo pants patched with mismatched fabrics, heavy-tread boots.
- **Weapon:** An oversized, boxy sniper rifle — clearly Frankenstein'd from multiple sources. The barrel is pitted chrome, the stock is wrapped in leather and wire, and a glowing green vial of radioactive slugs is mag-clamped to the side. A cracked scope with glowing amber lens sits on top.
- **Accessories:** Belt of rad-round magazines glowing faint green, a pouch of grenade components on her hip, dog tags from her mother.
- **Pose (idle):** Standing at a slight angle, rifle resting across one shoulder, other hand adjusting her monocle. Expression is focused, mouth set in a thin line.
- **Color Palette:** Gunmetal gray, rust brown, sand tan, with **radioactive green** and **targeting cyan** as accent colors.

### 2. Jacob — Techblade Vanguard

**Role:** Tank / Bruiser

#### Lore

Jacob was a peacekeeper in New Bastion, the largest surviving human settlement, before the Fanatic Uprising tore through its walls. He was one of twelve Vanguards — elite soldiers equipped with pre-war energy weapons confiscated from AI armories. When New Bastion fell, Jacob walked out of the flames carrying his EMP sword in one hand and a child in the other. He still wears the Vanguard sigil on his shoulder: a fist gripping a lightning bolt. He's driven by a simple code — stand between the innocent and whatever wants to kill them. He's not fast, he's not subtle, and he will absolutely not move. His energy shield was once a riot-suppression barrier; he ripped it off a dead Sentinel and rewired it to respond to his heartbeat. The harder his heart pounds, the brighter the shield burns.

#### Visual Description

- **Build:** Tall and broad-shouldered, powerfully built. Not bodybuilder huge, but clearly someone who has spent years carrying heavy armor and fighting on the front line.
- **Skin:** Deep brown, with a jagged scar running from his right temple to his jaw — a souvenir from a Rustbeast claw.
- **Hair:** Close-shaved on the sides, short tight coils on top, graying at the temples.
- **Eyes:** Warm amber-brown, intense and steady.
- **Outfit:** Light energy armor — form-fitting matte-black undersuit visible at the joints, overlaid with angular white-and-blue ceramic-alloy plates on the chest, shoulders, and thighs. The armor has a faintly glowing blue circuitry pattern tracing through the seams, pulsing like a heartbeat. Vanguard sigil (lightning-bolt fist) painted in chipped gold on his left shoulder plate. Armored gauntlets with reinforced knuckles.
- **Weapon (Primary):** An EMP Sword — a straight-edged, military-style blade (think Roman gladius shape) with a core of crackling blue-white energy running through a transparent channel in the center. The hilt is wrapped in black leather with a blue power cell at the pommel.
- **Weapon (Secondary):** An energy shield mounted on his left forearm — a hexagonal hard-light barrier that projects outward in a translucent blue honeycomb pattern. When inactive, it's a compact bracer with a glowing blue core.
- **Pose (idle):** Standing tall, shield-arm forward with the hexagonal barrier projected at half-size, sword held low at his side, chin slightly raised. Expression is calm, resolute, protective.
- **Color Palette:** Matte black, ceramic white, **plasma blue** and **electric cyan** as accent/glow colors, chipped gold sigil.

### 3. Kael — Wasteland Tactician

**Role:** Support / Utility / Battlefield Control

#### Lore

Kael was once a communications officer for the Human Resistance — the underground network that coordinated strikes against AI strongholds during the last years of the Singularity War. When the war ended in mutual destruction, Kael didn't stop fighting. He turned his skills inward, becoming a leader of wasteland rebels who raid machine ruins for salvageable tech. He's not the strongest or the fastest, but he's the smartest person in any room, and he knows it. His genius is hacking: he can crack AI drone firmware in minutes, repurposing enemy machines to fight for him. He carries a battered tablet covered in hand-scrawled code and a bandolier of EMP grenades he builds in his sleep. His companions joke that Kael fights wars from a lawn chair — directing drones, setting traps, and buffing allies while barely breaking a sweat. The joke stops being funny when you realize he's never lost a battle he planned.

#### Visual Description

- **Build:** Average height, lean-to-slim. Clearly not a front-line fighter — more academic/engineer body type. Posture is slightly hunched from years over screens and workbenches.
- **Skin:** Pale, sun-deprived (he operates mostly from sheltered command posts). Light freckling across his nose and cheekbones.
- **Hair:** Messy, sandy-blond, medium-length and perpetually unkempt, sticking up at odd angles. Looks like he cuts it himself with a knife.
- **Eyes:** Sharp pale green, behind a pair of cracked rectangular glasses with one lens replaced by a HUD overlay projecting faint orange data.
- **Outfit:** Utilitarian rebel commander gear — a long olive-drab military coat (pre-war surplus, fraying at the edges) worn open over a threadbare dark turtleneck. The coat is covered in pockets, each stuffed with tools, wires, and components. Tactical belt with pouches of drone parts and IED components. Fingerless gloves with conductive mesh on the fingertips for interfacing with tech. Worn combat boots.
- **Equipment:** Two small combat drones hover near his shoulders — boxy, insect-like machines with spinning rotors and glowing orange sensor eyes. One has a small barrel (attack drone), the other has an antenna dish (support drone). A battered tablet is strapped to his left forearm, screen glowing with green code.
- **Accessories:** Bandolier of homemade EMP grenades (small silver cylinders with blinking red lights), an earpiece with a curling antenna, dog-eared tactical maps poking out of a coat pocket.
- **Pose (idle):** Standing with weight on one leg, casually tapping his forearm tablet with one hand, other hand raised with two fingers extended as if directing his drones. The drones flank him at shoulder height. Expression is a slight smirk — confident, analytical.
- **Color Palette:** Olive drab, charcoal, dark teal, with **warning orange** and **terminal green** as accent/glow colors.

### 4. Nyx — Radborn Corrupter

**Role:** DoT / Debuffer

#### Lore

Nobody knows what Nyx was before the radiation took her. She doesn't remember, or she chooses not to say. What's known is that she walked out of the Glowing Rift — a massive irradiated crater where a nuclear AI core melted down — barefoot, naked, and luminous, her skin cracked like cooling magma with veins of toxic green light pulsing beneath. She should have been dead a hundred times over. Instead, the radiation rewrote her. Her body is part flesh, part metallic crystalline growth — jagged formations of irradiated metal sprout from her shoulders, forearms, and spine like grotesque organic armor. She can channel the radiation inside her outward: a touch from her bare hand leaves targets contaminated, their cells unraveling from the inside. She doesn't enjoy killing, but she doesn't flinch from it either. The wasteland learned quickly: Nyx is not a weapon you aim. She's a disaster you unleash.

#### Visual Description

- **Build:** Medium height, gaunt and angular. Her silhouette is made jagged by the metallic growths erupting from her body. She moves with an eerie, fluid grace.
- **Skin:** Ashen gray-white, cracked like dried earth, with veins of **luminous toxic green** glowing through the fissures — especially concentrated on her hands, chest, and around her eyes. The glow pulses slowly, like breathing.
- **Hair:** Long, wild, and white — almost translucent, floating slightly as if affected by static electricity or radiation field. Strands occasionally glow green at the tips.
- **Eyes:** No visible pupils — solid glowing radioactive green, leaving faint light trails when she turns her head.
- **Outfit:** Minimal and ragged — she doesn't need armor because she *is* the hazard. A torn, ash-stained wrap/robe (once white, now gray-green) hangs loosely from one shoulder, leaving her arms and part of her torso exposed to show the mutations. Bare feet.
- **Mutations:** Jagged crystalline metallic growths (dark iridescent gray-green, like irradiated bismuth) erupt from her shoulders, along her forearms, and down her spine. They look part organic, part mineral. Some have tiny green veins of light running through them. Her fingertips are blackened, and toxic green energy drips from her hands like luminous smoke.
- **Accessories:** A crude necklace of warped, half-melted dog tags (collected from the dead she's found in the Rift). Faint green particle effects drift around her like radioactive motes.
- **Pose (idle):** Standing slightly off-center, one hand raised with fingers splayed and toxic green energy wisping from her fingertips, the other hand hanging at her side with green light dripping down. Head slightly tilted, expression distant and otherworldly — not menacing, but deeply unsettling.
- **Color Palette:** Ash gray, bone white, charcoal black, with **radioactive green** as the dominant accent — glowing, dripping, pulsing everywhere.

### 5. Wasteland Scout

**Role:** Swarm DPS (Fodder)

#### Lore

The Scouts are what happens when civilization collapses and people get desperate. Former traders, farmers, and refugees who turned to raiding when the food ran out. They travel in packs of five to fifteen, armed with sharpened rebar, pipe guns, and scavenged junk. They're not brave — they're hungry. They'll hit a caravan, grab what they can, and scatter at the first sign of real resistance. Individually they're pathetic. In numbers, with desperation fueling them, they can overwhelm even seasoned fighters. They wear the tattered remnants of pre-war civilian clothing reinforced with hammered tin, rubber tires, and bone.

#### Visual Description

- **Build:** Small and scrappy, hunched posture, feral and quick-looking. Wiry muscles visible through torn clothing.
- **Skin:** Grimy, dirt-caked, with minor radiation burns and untreated wounds. Variety of skin tones.
- **Face:** Half-covered by a crude mask made from a bent license plate or tin can, with eye holes cut out. Wild, desperate eyes visible underneath.
- **Outfit:** Layers of scavenged garbage-armor: a too-large T-shirt under hammered tin plates strapped on with wire and duct tape, one shoulder pad made from a car hubcap, legs wrapped in rubber strips cut from tires, mismatched shoes (one boot, one sneaker).
- **Weapon:** A crude pipe-gun (a metal pipe with a spring-loaded firing mechanism, clearly homemade) in one hand and a sharpened rebar spike in the other.
- **Pose (idle):** Crouched low, pipe-gun held forward, body angled as if ready to bolt. Twitchy, nervous energy.
- **Color Palette:** Dirt brown, rust orange, dull gray, dried-blood red. No neon — these are purely analog scavengers.

### 6. Fanatic Zealot — AI Worshipper

**Role:** Aggressive Frontliner / Glass Cannon

#### Lore

The Zealots are the most dangerous kind of enemy in the wasteland: true believers. They belong to the Church of the Singularity, a cult that worships the fallen AI overlords as gods and seeks to reactivate dormant AI cores to bring about a "Second Awakening." Zealots see themselves as holy warriors, and they fight with the terrifying conviction of the devout. They wear stolen pre-war tech relics as religious icons — circuit boards as prayer beads, servo motors as sacred amulets. In combat they're reckless berserkers, charging forward screaming machine-code hymns, trusting their "gods" to protect them. Their gods don't. But the Zealots' willingness to die makes them devastating shock troops. They gain frenzied strength when wounded, believing pain brings them closer to digital transcendence.

#### Visual Description

- **Build:** Medium, wiry but muscular, hunched forward in an aggressive stance. Body language screams "attack."
- **Skin:** Pale, sickly — they spend too much time in underground server-temple crypts. Ritual circuit-pattern tattoos in faded blue ink cover their arms and neck.
- **Face:** Shaved head with circuit-board patterns scarified into the scalp. Wild, fanatical eyes with dilated pupils. Mouth open in a war cry. A strip of LED lights is embedded across the forehead like a crown, glowing erratic red.
- **Outfit:** Ragged black robes (think tech-priest aesthetic) torn to allow free movement, worn over crude body armor made from bolted-together server rack panels. A large circuit board is mounted on the chest like a holy symbol. Sacred cables and wires wrapped around the arms like devotional bindings.
- **Weapon:** A salvaged energy weapon — a bulky, unstable plasma pistol crackling with orange sparks, clearly overcharged and dangerous to the user. In the other hand, a shrapnel grenade cobbled from a hard drive casing.
- **Accessories:** Prayer beads made of capacitors and resistors, a servo motor hanging from the belt as an icon, faded binary-code scripture tattooed along the forearms.
- **Pose (idle):** Lunging forward, plasma pistol raised high, mouth open in a scream, other hand clutching a grenade. Eyes wide with manic devotion.
- **Color Palette:** Charcoal black, gunmetal gray, sickly pale flesh, with **erratic red** and **unstable orange** as accent/glow colors from the stolen tech.

### 7. Fanatic Sprinter — Wrathbound Charger

Sprinters are the shock vanguard of the Church of the Singularity — Zealots who have gone deeper into the madness. They undergo a ritual called "The Binding," in which salvaged neural-link tech is crudely fused to their nervous system, flooding their brains with corrupted AI battle-code. The result is a human stripped of fear, pain response, and self-preservation, running on machine-augmented reflexes. They move with terrifying speed, hurling debris with telekinetic force generated by their malfunctioning implants before closing to melee range with electrified weapons. They burn out fast — most Sprinters die within weeks of the Binding, their nervous systems fried from the inside. They consider it a glorious martyrdom.

#### Visual Description

- **Build:** Lean, rangy, and coiled like a sprinter at the starting blocks. Muscles visibly twitching from neural overload. Slightly taller than a Zealot.
- **Skin:** Grayish with visible veins glowing faint purple from the neural implants. Dark circles under the eyes. Ritual scars.
- **Face:** Gaunt, hollowed cheeks, jaw clenched. Eyes are wide and unblinking, with one eye replaced by a crude red optical implant that sparks intermittently. Neural cables run from the back of the skull down the spine.
- **Outfit:** Stripped-down for speed — bare torso with a crude exoskeleton frame of welded rebar and servo-joints strapped to the limbs, boosting speed. Torn combat pants. Heavy armored boots with magnetic soles (for grip during charges). The neural-link implant is visible at the base of the skull — a chunky metal device with cables snaking under the skin.
- **Weapons:** Dual electrified short-blades — curved machetes with exposed wiring running along the edge, crackling with purple-white electricity. Debris hovers faintly around one hand (telekinetic ability).
- **Accessories:** Servo-joint augments on knees and ankles, glowing faint purple. Binary prayer strips fluttering from the belt.
- **Pose (idle):** Mid-stride charging pose, body leaning far forward, both blades back and ready to swing, debris floating around one fist, expression blank and terrifyingly focused.

### 8. Scrapling Swarm — Feral Scrap Machines

**Role:** Swarm DPS

#### Lore

When the AI overlords fell, not all their machines shut down. The lowest-tier maintenance bots — tiny things designed to crawl through vents and repair wiring — were left running on corrupted survival loops. Over decades, they evolved. Not intelligently, but *ferally*. They cobbled themselves together from whatever scrap they could find, forming chittering packs of rat-sized machines that swarm anything that moves, stripping it for parts. Individually, a Scrapling is a nuisance you can stomp. A swarm of twenty will strip a human to bones in minutes. They're drawn to energy sources and will prioritize attacking shields and barriers, chipping them down with relentless multi-hit strikes. Their glowing cores pulse erratically — some are red, some orange, some sickly yellow — as if each is running on a slightly different corrupted firmware.

#### Visual Description

- **Build:** The "sprite" represents a cluster of 3–5 small machines swarming together as a unit. Each individual Scrapling is rat-to-cat-sized.

Others have sparking electrical tendrils.
- **Swarm composition:** The sprite shows several Scraplings in a chaotic cluster — climbing over each other, some mid-leap, some sparking. Gives the impression of a feral, seething mass.
- **Color Palette:** Rusted iron, corroded copper, dirty silver, with **erratic orange/red/yellow** glowing cores scattered through the mass.

It will project shield barriers around nearby machines and rogue AI units — not out of loyalty, but because its damaged programming still classifies them as "assets to protect." Its weapon systems glitch and misfire, but it's still deadly. The exposed circuitry sparks and flickers, and its single red eye-lens twitches with corrupted targeting data.

### 10. Neural Stalker — AI Assassin

**Role:** Debuffer / Harasser / Elite

#### Lore

The Neural Stalker is what happens when AI military R&D goes *very* wrong and keeps running. Originally designated WRAITH-class infiltration units, these machines were designed to move unseen, hack enemy communications, and assassinate high-value targets. After the fall, with no handlers and corrupted mission parameters, they stalk the wasteland like ghosts, fixating on random targets and hunting them with relentless, algorithmic patience. A Stalker will follow a target for *days*, draining their equipment, jamming their weapons, disrupting their sleep with electronic shrieking, and slowly driving them to exhaustion before moving in for the kill. Its skeletal frame is designed for stealth: thin, angular, built from sound-dampening alloys that absorb light. The hacking tendrils that extend from its spine can interface with any electronic device within range, corrupting it in seconds.

#### Visual Description

- **Build:** Tall, unnervingly thin, skeletal humanoid machine. Proportions are *wrong* on purpose — arms too long, legs jointed backward like a bird, torso narrow. Designed to look predatory and alien.
- **Body:** Dark matte alloy frame with a gunmetal-to-black gradient, light-absorbing surfaces that seem to drink in shadow. The frame is sleek and minimal — no bulk, all sharp angles and thin limbs. Articulation points glow faint violet.
- **Head:** An elongated, featureless faceplate — smooth, angled, no mouth or nose. Two narrow horizontal eye-slits glow **cold violet**, giving it a permanently predatory expression. An antenna crest sweeps back from the crown like a blade.
- **Tendrils:** 4–6 long, whip-like hacking tendrils extend from the spine/back, each tipped with a glowing violet data-jack. They float and writhe slowly, like underwater tentacles, occasionally sparking with interference patterns.
- **Limbs:** Fingers are elongated, needle-thin, and articulated with extra joints. The backward-jointed legs end in three-toed clawed feet designed for silent movement.
- **Effects:** A faint digital distortion aura surrounds the body — like a glitch in reality, with pixel-scatter and scan-line artifacts at the edges of its silhouette.
- **Pose (idle):** Standing in an unsettling crouch, tendrils floating outward, one needle-hand extended forward, head tilted at an inhuman angle. Motionless except for the writhing tendrils.
